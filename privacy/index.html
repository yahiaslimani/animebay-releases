Privacy policy

AnimeBay · last updated 27 November 2026

AnimeBay is an anime tracker for Android. It works without an account and keeps your data on your phone. This page explains what leaves the phone and why.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings, watch history and lists kept in the app.
  • AniList and MyAnimeList sign-in tokens, if you connect an account. They are only sent to AniList or MyAnimeList, to read and update your lists.

Services AnimeBay talks to

  • AniList (anilist.co) and, if you sign in, MyAnimeList: show information, schedules and your lists. Their own privacy policies apply to your accounts there.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (Aptabase): which screens and features are used, the app version, and the country, with no account, name or advertising ID. You can turn this off in More → Share usage stats.
  • Crash reports (Sentry): off unless you turn them on. They contain technical details of the crash, not your lists or accounts.
  • Ads (Unity Ads): a banner on the Home screen. Unity may use your device's advertising ID and IP address to show and measure ads; see Unity's privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

What AnimeBay does not collect

No name, email address, contacts, location, photos or files. AnimeBay has no accounts of its own and sells no data.

Children

AnimeBay is not designed for children under 13.

Deleting your data

Sign out of AniList or MyAnimeList in More, or clear AnimeBay's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Data held by AniList, MyAnimeList or Unity is managed in those services.
