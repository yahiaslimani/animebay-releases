# AnimeBay for Android

Download AnimeBay here. After the first install, the app keeps itself up to date: new versions download quietly in the background, so you only need this page once.

## Install

1. Open **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** on your phone and download the file for your phone:
   - **`AnimeBay-…-arm64-v8a.apk`**: almost every phone from the last ten years. Pick this one if you are not sure.
   - `AnimeBay-…-armeabi-v7a.apk`: older 32-bit phones only.
   - `AnimeBay-…-x86_64.apk`: Android emulators on a computer.
2. Open the downloaded file. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser or file app: tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back and tap **Install**.
3. Open AnimeBay. That's it.

If Android says the app "isn't installed", check you picked `arm64-v8a`, and that you have about 150 MB free.

## Updates

AnimeBay checks for new versions by itself and downloads them in the background, pausing while you watch.

- **Android 12 and newer:** the update installs when you leave the app. Next time you open AnimeBay, it is the new version.
- **Android 11 and older:** Android always asks before installing. When an update is ready, **More** shows **Update ready · Install** (with a dot on the More tab). One tap, then confirm.

You never need to uninstall to update. Your history, lists and downloads stay.

## Privacy

AnimeBay has no account of its own. If you connect AniList, your lists sync with your AniList account. Crash reports are **off** unless you turn them on in **More**; they are anonymous and help fix problems faster.
