Install in a minute

AnimeBay isn't on the Play Store, so Android asks once before installing it.

Download

Tap Download for Android on your phone. The file is about 27 MB.

Allow and install

Open the file. Android asks to allow installs from your browser: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install.

Watch

Open AnimeBay, pick subbed or dubbed, and you're watching. You only do this once: updates come by themselves.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. AnimeBay is free and has no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. You can keep your list on your phone. Connecting AniList is optional and brings your lists and progress, and every episode you finish is saved there.

Which phones does it work on?

Android 8 or newer. The download button gets the version for almost every phone from the last ten years. If it doesn't install, try the file for older 32-bit phones.

How do updates work?

AnimeBay downloads new versions in the background and pauses while you watch. On Android 12 and newer it installs when you leave the app; on older Android, tap Update ready · Install in More.

What about my privacy?

AnimeBay has no account of its own. Crash reports are off unless you turn them on in More; they're anonymous and help fix problems faster.
